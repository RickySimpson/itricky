Questions before
you start?

How does the website help me get more business?

It gives people who find your business a clear view of what you offer, reasons to choose you, and an easy way to contact you. We’ll build around the action that matters to your business, such as a call or service inquiry, so visitors know what to do next.

What does the $500 cover?

It covers the design and build of your one-page website, up to six sections, revisions before launch, and connection to your existing domain. You work directly with me throughout. Domain registration and paid third-party services are separate; ongoing care is optional.

Do I need the $20/month plan?

No. Care is optional. If you prefer to manage the site yourself, we’ll agree on the hosting setup and handoff before work begins. You can discuss care with me later, too.

Can you use my current domain?

Yes. You keep ownership of your domain. We’ll arrange the access needed to connect the new site and preserve your existing email settings.

What will you need from me?

Your business details, services, contact information, and any logo or photos you want to use. If something is missing, tell me—we’ll work out what’s needed before starting.

How long will it take?

I’ll deliver your one-page website within one week of receiving your content. We’ll confirm what you need to send before starting. The site goes live after your review and approval.

What costs extra?

Domain registration and paid third-party services are separate. Additional pages, online stores, custom booking systems, and larger features need a separate quote. We’ll agree on what your project needs before work begins.
